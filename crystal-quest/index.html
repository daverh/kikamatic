Crystal Quest

Coming soon to Google Play

Join a brave hero with a diamond sword on a quest for the magic crystals. There's just one problem: the crystals are guarded by dinosaurs!

Crystal Quest is a bright cartoon platformer for kids aged about 5 to 12 and their families. The story, the hero and the dinosaurs were invented by Oliver and Nick, two young game designers.

Four levels, four bosses

  • Coconut Island

    Whack palm trees for coconuts, then face the crowned T-Rex. When he stomps, jump!

  • The Volcano

    Leap over lava, dodge pterodactyls and falling rocks, then beat the Lava Boss.

  • Frozen Mountain

    Slide on slippery ice and hit the Ice Rex's snowballs back with your sword.

  • The Lost Temple

    Ride moving platforms, light up a dark bat cave and crack the Temple Guardian's armour.

More levels are on the way!

What's in the game

  • Keep your power up with coconuts, and look out for golden coconuts (super speed) and shield bubbles.
  • Find 3 hidden stars in every level, and use checkpoint flags and signposts along the way.
  • A timer keeps your best times, so the whole family can race each other.
  • Easy mode with extra hearts for younger players.
  • Music for every level, big touch buttons, and game controller support.

Made for kids and families

  • Cartoon action only: beaten dinosaurs just spin away with a cheerful "Bye bye dino!"
  • No internet needed, so it's great for car trips and holidays.
