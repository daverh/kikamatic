Crystal Quest – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Crystal Quest handles information. The short version: we don't collect anything. The game has no ads, no purchases, no accounts, and it never connects to the internet.

Who we are

Crystal Quest is made by Kikamatic, an independent game studio in Australia. Story and game design are by Oliver and Nick. Programming is by Kikamatic.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Kikamatic. "The game" means Crystal Quest on any device.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not share or sell any information.
  • The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts or logins.
  • The game never uses the internet. It does not send or receive anything.
  • The game asks for no special permissions (no location, camera, microphone or contacts). The only permission it has is to make the phone vibrate, and you can turn that off.

What the game saves on your device

To make the game work, it saves a few small files on your device only:

  • your best time for each level
  • which levels are unlocked
  • which stars you've found
  • your settings: sound on or off, music and sound volume, Easy mode, vibration and screen shake

These files sit in the game's private storage on your device. Other apps can't read them. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We can't see them, and we don't know who plays the game.

None of this information identifies you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect names, email addresses, ages, photos, voice recordings, location, device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage statistics or crash reports.

What we share or sell

Nothing. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share or sell.

Children's privacy

Crystal Quest is made for children and families. We built it so children can play safely without giving away any information.

  • Children are never asked for personal information.
  • There is no chat, no messaging and no way to talk to other players.
  • Players can't create or share content.
  • There are no links out of the game, no ads and nothing to buy.

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, we have nothing to ask parental consent for. We aim for the game to sit comfortably with children's privacy laws such as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the children's protections in the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Australian Privacy Act 1988. If you're a parent or carer and you have any question, please get in touch (see "Contact us" below).

Permissions

The game has one permission: vibrate, so the phone can buzz gently when the hero is hit. Android grants it automatically and it gives the game no access to any information. You can turn vibration off in the game's pause menu.

The game can't use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files outside its own storage.

Third parties

There are no third-party services in the game. No advertising networks, analytics tools or crash-reporting services are included.

The game is built with the Godot Engine, an open-source game engine. In Crystal Quest, Godot does not collect or send any data.

When you download the game from an app store (for example Google Play), that store may collect information under its own privacy policy. That is between you and the store, and we don't receive any of that information about you.

How to delete your data

All game data is on your device. To delete it, simply uninstall Crystal Quest. This removes your best times, unlocked levels, stars and settings.

You can also clear the game's data in your device's settings (on Android: Settings → Apps → Crystal Quest → Storage → Clear storage). The exact menu names depend on your device.

We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Security

Your game data never leaves your device, so it is protected by your device's own security. We recommend keeping your device's software up to date.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. If we ever planned to start collecting information, we would update this policy before that change and, where the law requires it, ask for consent first.
