Enologica Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Enologica is a wine cellar app made by Kikamatic, an independent developer in Australia. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. (Kikamatic's games have their own privacy policy.)

The short version

  • Your cellar, tasting notes, wish list and photos are stored on your phone.
  • There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Information leaves your phone only if you set up an AI service with your own key and ask it for help. It then goes directly to that service, not to us.
  • We don't receive, sell or share any of your information.

What Enologica stores on your phone

Your wines, vintages, bottles and storage locations, tasting notes and ratings, your wish list, photos you add, your settings, and any AI service keys you enter. AI keys are kept in Android's encrypted storage. This data stays in Enologica's private storage on your phone; other apps can't read it, and we can't see it.

Reading the text on a wine label happens on your phone (using Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device). The photo is not sent anywhere for this.

Optional AI features

Enologica can use an AI service for cellaring advice, drinking windows, buying ideas and richer label scans. This is off until you add your own key for Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic or Perplexity in Settings.

When you then ask for AI help, Enologica sends what that request needs directly from your phone to the service you chose, over an encrypted connection:

  • for a label scan: the label photo;
  • for advice, recommendations or a summary: details of the relevant wines from your cellar (for example names, producers, vintages, varieties and your notes).

That service handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy, through your own account with it. We don't receive a copy. You can remove a key at any time in Settings, and nothing is sent once it's gone.

  • Google Gemini: policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenAI: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic: anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Perplexity: perplexity.ai/hub/legal/privacy-policy

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph wine labels and bottles, only when you choose to.
  • Internet: only for the optional AI features.

Choosing a photo from your gallery uses Android's photo picker, which gives Enologica only the photo you pick, not access to your other photos.

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export your cellar from the app at any time. To delete everything, uninstall Enologica or clear its storage (on Android: Settings → Apps → Enologica → Storage → Clear storage). We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete. For data sent to an AI service, use that service's own account settings.

Adults only

Enologica is about wine and is intended for adults of legal drinking age. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Third parties

When you download Enologica from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy; we don't receive it. Apart from the AI services you choose to set up, Enologica includes no third-party services that receive your data.

Changes to this policy

If Enologica starts handling information differently (for example when optional sign-in and sync across devices arrive), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date.
