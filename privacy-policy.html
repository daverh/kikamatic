Kikamatic Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how games made by Kikamatic handle information. It covers every Kikamatic game on every device. The section "Our games" lists each game and anything that is specific to it.

Who we are

Kikamatic is an independent game studio in Australia. Our games are designed by young game designers and programmed by Kikamatic.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Kikamatic. "Our games" means any game published by Kikamatic.

The short version

  • Our games do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not share or sell any information.
  • Our games have no accounts or logins, and children are never asked for personal information.
  • Game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • If a game ever changes this, we will update this policy before that version is released.

What our games save on your device

To work properly, a game may save small files on your device only, such as best times, unlocked levels, items found and settings like sound volume. The details for each game are listed under "Our games".

These files sit in the game's private storage on your device. Other apps can't read them. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We can't see them, and we don't know who plays our games. None of this information identifies you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect names, email addresses, ages, photos, voice recordings, location, device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage statistics or crash reports.

What we share or sell

Nothing. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share or sell.

Children's privacy

Our games are made for children and families, and we build them so children can play safely without giving away any information.

  • Children are never asked for personal information.
  • There is no chat, no messaging and no way to talk to other players.
  • Players can't create or share content with others.

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children, we have nothing to ask parental consent for. We aim for our games to sit comfortably with children's privacy laws such as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the children's protections in the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Australian Privacy Act 1988. If you're a parent or carer and you have any question, please get in touch (see "Contact us" below).

Third parties

Unless a game's section below says otherwise, our games include no third-party services: no advertising networks, analytics tools or crash-reporting services.

Our games are built with the Godot Engine, an open-source game engine. In our games, Godot does not collect or send any data.

When you download a game from an app store (for example Google Play), that store may collect information under its own privacy policy. That is between you and the store, and we don't receive any of that information about you.

Our games

Crystal Quest

  • Ads and purchases: none.
  • Internet: the game never uses the internet. It does not send or receive anything.
  • Saved on your device: your best time for each level, which levels are unlocked, which stars you've found, and your settings (sound on or off, music and sound volume, Easy mode, vibration and screen shake).
  • Permissions: only vibrate, so the phone can buzz gently when the hero is hit. Android grants it automatically and it gives the game no access to any information. You can turn vibration off in the game's pause menu. The game can't use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files outside its own storage.

Robot Garden

  • Ads and purchases: none. Coins are earned by playing and can only be spent inside the game on paint colours, hats and stickers for the robot.
  • Internet: the game never uses the internet. It does not send or receive anything.
  • Saved on your device: which levels are unlocked, the items found and puzzles solved in each level, the last charging pad reached, upgrades, coins, lives, the robot's paint colour, hat and sticker, and whether sound is on or off. Choosing New game on the title screen clears it.
  • Permissions: only vibrate, so the phone can buzz gently when the robot is caught or bumps the Skeleton King. Android grants it automatically and it gives the game no access to any information. Turning sound off in the game's pause menu turns vibration off too. The game can't use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files outside its own storage.

How to delete your data

All game data is on your device. To delete it, simply uninstall the game. This removes everything it saved.

You can also clear a game's data in your device's settings (on Android: Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear storage). The exact menu names depend on your device.

We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Security

Your game data never leaves your device, so it is protected by your device's own security. We recommend keeping your device's software up to date.

Changes to this policy

When we release a new game, or a game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top. If a game were ever to start collecting information, we would update this policy before releasing that version and, where the law requires it, ask for consent first.
